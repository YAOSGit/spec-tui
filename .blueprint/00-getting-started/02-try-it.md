# Try It Out

## What to expect

Run spec-tui against the Petstore v3 spec to see it in action. The action on the right will build and launch the TUI pointing at the public Petstore API.

## How to interact

Once running, use arrow keys to browse endpoints in the navigator pane, press `Enter` to view details, and `Tab` to cycle between panes. Press `q` to quit.

## What to do

Press `r` to execute the action.
